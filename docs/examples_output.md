## Significant-digit grouping on a synthetic sample

N=10000 draws from a lognormal, generated with numpy default_rng(1).
This is full double precision, so grouping at sig=1..9 can be seen.
mode='sig5' is an alias for sig=5.
If grouping at that sig yields fewer than k groups, precision is raised (up to 15, then raw atoms) so k is kept when the raw data has at least k distinct values.
Result.sig is the sig actually used (0 if the fallback reached raw atoms). Dprime is the number of groups after that grouping.
